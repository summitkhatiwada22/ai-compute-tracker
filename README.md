# AI Compute Tracker

A public data tracker for the economics of the AI infrastructure buildout — part of an independent research practice covering AI capex, compute costs, and their intersection with monetary policy and capital markets.

## Series tracked

1. **Hyperscaler capex guidance** — quarterly capex guidance and actuals from Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta, sourced from SEC EDGAR filings and earnings call transcripts.
2. **GPU/compute pricing proxy** — spot and on-demand GPU rental pricing across providers, tracked via the Vast.ai API, as a proxy for compute cost trends.
3. **AI funding volume** — venture funding into AI companies, sourced from Crunchbase News and the PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor.
